# Pricing and Billing

On Amezmo you pay only for what you use, so every instance you launch is billed
by the hour. The [pricing page](/pricing) lists prices per month, but the
underlying basis is hourly. That way your card is charged at the same time each
month, once for every instance you ran during the prior month.

## In This Section

### Hourly Billing

[Hourly billing](hourly.md) explains the billing period: your card is charged on
the first day of the month for every hour an instance was active during the
prior month.

### Payment Methods

Add cards, choose a default, and remove ones you no longer use from
[Billing > Payment methods](payment-methods.md). Amezmo charges the default
card.

### Advanced Pay

[Advanced Pay](advanced-pay.md) funds an account balance up front, which usage
draws down as you incur it. Keep a backup payment method so instances aren't
terminated when the balance reaches zero.

### Credits

[Billing credits](credits.md) arrive as a promotion code and reduce the amount
due on your invoices until you spend them in full or they expire.

### Evaluation Period

New accounts get a [free trial](evaluation-period.md) with unlimited deploys.
When it ends, add a payment method to keep your application hosted.
